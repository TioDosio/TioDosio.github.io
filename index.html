About Me

Hi there! I'm currently pursuing my Master’s in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico, with a focus on Control, Robotics, and AI.

But that’s not the whole picture, I’m not just an (almost) engineer.

What really drives me is curiosity. I’m endlessly fascinated by how things work, whether it’s a complex control system or the sheer magic of how planes stay in the air (seriously, it still feels like wizardry). I love channeling that curiosity into hands-on learning, solving real-world problems, and building smarter, more efficient solutions.

In my free time, when I’m not coding or tinkering with a new idea, you’ll probably find me trying to automate something I absolutely could’ve done faster by hand. But hey, where’s the fun in that?
