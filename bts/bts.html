February 2025

Master Thesis and Skill Improvement

After returning from the Erasmus program at the Warsaw University of Technology, I started my Master's Thesis in the area of Social Robotics. This exciting project allows me to combine my interests in human-computer interaction, machine learning, and robotics.

Alongside my thesis work, I've been focusing on developing important technical skills through various platforms:

  • Completing cybersecurity exercises on TryHackMe to understand security fundamentals
  • Learning the basics of SQL for better database management and data analysis
  • Building this personal website to showcase my portfolio and improve my web development skills

The journey of building this website has been particularly rewarding, as it's allowed me to apply design principles while creating a platform to share my work with the world. I've learned a lot about responsive design, CSS organization, and the importance of user experience.

January 2024

Erasmus Experience in Warsaw

My Erasmus semester at the Warsaw University of Technology was an incredible experience that broadened my horizons both academically and personally. I had the opportunity to study in a different educational system, collaborate with international students, and experience a new culture.

During my time in Warsaw, I took specialized courses that complemented my studies at Instituto Superior Técnico, gaining new perspectives on robotics and AI applications. The international environment fostered cross-cultural collaboration and helped me develop a global mindset.

Beyond academics, living abroad taught me adaptability, independence, and cultural awareness - skills that will be valuable throughout my career. I'm grateful for the connections I made and the experiences that shaped my personal and professional growth.

Tags: Erasmus, International Experience, Academic Exchange

August 2024

Machine Learning at MavenPet

My summer internship at MavenPet was a valuable opportunity to apply my machine learning knowledge to real-world problems. I worked on feature engineering for their machine learning models, which are used to analyze pet behavior and health patterns.

The experience taught me about the challenges of working with real-world data, including handling missing values, identifying relevant features, and optimizing model performance. I also gained insights into the product development cycle and how machine learning integrates with other aspects of a technology business.

One of the most rewarding aspects was seeing how our models could potentially improve pet health outcomes by identifying patterns that might not be immediately obvious to pet owners or veterinarians. This experience reinforced my interest in applying AI to solve meaningful problems.

Tags: Machine Learning, Internship, Feature Engineering, Data Science
